## Unicamp honors athletes from JUBs 2026

On Thursday afternoon, Unicamp athletes who took part in the Brazilian University Games received diplomas at the Funcamp auditorium. The delegation competed in Goiânia and Trindade, Goiás, between August and September 2026.

Rector Paulo Cesar Montagner called for a robust, inclusive and lasting sports project, with universities as the foundation of sport. Sylvia Furegatti, Odilon Roble, Eduardo Calel Brandão, João Paulo Borin and Campinas municipal sports secretary Fernando Vanin also attended. Rodrigo Marques Dias recalled the six fights leading to the final, while Pablo Carneiro de Matos Viana highlighted the delegation and medal records.
