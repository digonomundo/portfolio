## Unicamp sets a medal record at JUBs 2026

Organized by CBDU in Goiás from August 22 to September 5, the competition brought results in jiu-jitsu, tennis, karate, taekwondo, eSports and the Academic category. The campaign was supported by the Directorate of Sports and the Pro-Rectory for Extension, Sport and Culture (ProEEC).

Rodrigo Marques Dias, a Telecommunications Engineering student at FT/Limeira, won gold in jiu-jitsu. It was his first flight and Unicamp men’s jiu-jitsu first title. Rayla Moura Sales won silver in women’s jiu-jitsu; Vitória de Brito Francisco took silver in tennis; Júlia Bicudo Vasconcelos won bronze in karate; and Mislleny Cristina Traqui da Silva won bronze in taekwondo. The Brawl Stars team — Guilherme Rodrigo Santana Tavares, Gabriel Bacoccina Motta and Bruno Bonet Gonçalves — earned silver in eSports. Pamela Izabela Peres competed in the Academic category, while Mayumi Godoy Ikeda returned to swimming after seven years.
