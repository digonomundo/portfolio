## Rodrigo Marques Dias wins gold at JUBs 2026

Rodrigo Marques Dias, a Telecommunications Engineering student at FT/Limeira, won gold in jiu-jitsu at JUBs 2026. It was his first title at the competition and the first gold medal for Unicamp men’s jiu-jitsu.

The achievement came during the university’s record campaign in Goiás, with 25 athletes, eight medals and two trophies. More than an individual result, the title reflects the meeting of university sport, discipline and the experience of representing Unicamp.
